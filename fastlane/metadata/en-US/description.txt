Fairway Ledger is a golf practice journal and stat book, finished in black and gold.

Log your range sessions ball by ball, write down what clicked, keep your scorecards hole by hole, and watch the numbers tell you where to practise next.

AT THE RANGE
• Pick full swing, wedges, chipping, pitching, bunker, putting or on-course practice
• Choose the club and target, then tap each shot: pure, solid or poor strike
• Count shots on target, ball shape (hook, draw, straight, fade, slice) and misses (left, right, short, long, fat, thin, topped, shank)
• Log carry distances and see your real average per club
• Putting: makes and misses by distance, where you missed (low side, high side, short, long), and the tour make rate from that distance
• Undo any tap

YOUR JOURNAL
• Rate the session, your mood and energy
• Swing thought, what clicked, what to work on next, and free notes
• Search every entry, filter by practice type

ROUNDS
• Hole-by-hole scorecard: score, putts, tee shot, penalties, greenside bunkers
• Greens in regulation, scrambling, sand saves and three-putts worked out for you
• Handicap Index estimate from your best rounds (World Handicap System style)

STATS
• Score trend, fairways, greens, putts, scrambling
• Putts holed by distance compared with the tour
• Strike quality, shot shape and miss direction, with a plain-English read of your pattern
• Where your practice time goes, and minutes per week

YARDAGE BOOK
• Carry and total for every club, a gapping chart that flags gaps and overlaps, and your range average next to each club

Goals, a weekly practice target and streaks keep you coming back. No account, no ads, no tracking: everything stays on your phone.
