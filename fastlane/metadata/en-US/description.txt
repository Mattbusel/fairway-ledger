Fairway Ledger is a golf practice journal, scorecard and stat book, finished in black and gold. Log your range sessions ball by ball, keep your scorecards hole by hole, and let your own numbers tell you where to practise next.

ON THE COURSE
• Hole-by-hole scorecard: score, putts, tee shot, penalties, greenside bunkers
• Saved courses: pars, rating and slope fill in by themselves next time
• 18 holes, front nine or back nine
• Your round on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island: hole, thru, score to par
• Greens in regulation, scrambling, sand saves and three-putts worked out for you
• Handicap index estimate (World Handicap System style), with your course handicap and net score on every card

AT THE RANGE
• Full swing, wedges, chipping, pitching, bunker, putting or on-course practice
• Tap each shot: pure, solid or poor; shape; on target; misses; carry distances
• Putting makes and misses by distance, next to the tour's make rate
• A weekly practice plan built from your own rounds: the clubs, targets and putts that cost you strokes, timed to about an hour

A NEW CHALLENGE EVERY MONTH
• Zero three-putts, six greens, eight range sessions, a clean card and more
• Progress tracks itself from what you log, and every finished month earns a medal for your cabinet

WIDGETS, CONTROLS AND SIRI
• Handicap widget for the Home Screen, the Lock Screen and StandBy
• Practice widget with buttons that log 15 or 30 minutes without opening the app
• Control Center buttons to start practice, log a round or add practice time
• Siri and Shortcuts: "Start practice in Fairway Ledger", "What's my handicap in Fairway Ledger"

YOUR JOURNAL
• Rate the session, your mood and energy
• Swing thought, what clicked, what to work on next

FAIRWAY LEDGER PRO (one-time purchase)
• Score trend across every round
• Where the strokes go: over par on par 3s, 4s and 5s, penalties and three-putts per round
• Every course, hole by hole: the holes that cost you and the ones to attack
• Putting against the tour, ball flight read, practice mix
• The Ledger widget: your last ten rounds, putts, fairways and greens
• Export rounds and sessions as spreadsheet files
